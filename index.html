    Good questions

    Before your first class

    Do I need to be fit or have experience?

    No. Beginners and mixed-levels classes are built for people starting from scratch. Advanced classes and sparring are by coach invite, so nobody gets thrown in at the deep end.

    What should I wear and bring?

    Comfortable gym kit and a bottle of water. Ask the coach about gloves and kit on your first visit.

    How do I book?

    Buy the 5-class intro pass, then book each class in advance in the TeamUp app. Can’t make it? Cancel at least 4 hours before.

    Is there a women-only class?

    Yes — Ladies Muay Thai runs on Wednesdays at 6pm.

    Can I watch my child’s class?

    Yes. There’s a designated viewing area so parents can watch every session.
